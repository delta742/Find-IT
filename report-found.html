Guidelines for Finders

  • High-Value Items: For laptops, phones, wallets, or government IDs, surrender them to the nearest Campus Security Desk or Faculty Office.
  • Protect Confidential Details: Do not publicly publish full card numbers or sensitive student documents; let the claiming student describe these to confirm ownership.
  • Check Lost Reports: A student might have already filed a matching report. Check Lost Reports.

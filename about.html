About FindIT

A centralized lost and found digital management system engineered for university campuses, replacing scattered notice boards and chat groups.

The FindIT Mission

On a bustling university campus, students and staff frequently misplace critical belongings—student IDs, laptops, keys, textbooks, and earphones. Traditionally, notices are scattered across physical boards or buried in ephemeral WhatsApp chat threads where information is lost within hours.

FindIT provides a single, structured, and searchable repository. With real-time status tracking, smart category/keyword comparison, and privacy-protected claim workflows, FindIT reduces lost item recovery time from days to minutes.

Key System Capabilities

  • Automated Matchmaking: Proactively pairs lost reports with found reports using category, timestamp proximity, and location heuristics.
  • Secure Claim Protocol: Enables rightful owners to describe private identifying marks before handover.
  • Account Verification: Restricts reporting and claims to registered campus members to maintain community trust.
  • Zero Setup Requirement: Client-side browser storage architecture allows immediate deployment and demonstration without complex database dependencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my reported data stored?

In this frontend demonstration, listings, user accounts, and messages are persisted in your browser's LocalStorage and SessionStorage. Other devices will not see your local changes unless connected to an integrated backend.

How does the automated matching algorithm work?

When an item is viewed, FindIT computes a match score (0–100%) against opposing open reports by scoring shared keyword tokens in names and descriptions (+30%), identical campus locations (+15%), and timestamp proximity (+15%).

What should I do if I find a high-value item?

For laptops, smartphones, debit cards, and official ID cards, hand them over to Central Campus Security or the MCA Department office, and specify that location in your report description.

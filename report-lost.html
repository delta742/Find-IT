Reporting Best Practices

  • Check existing found listings: Someone may have already submitted your item to the security desk. Visit Found Items first.
  • Note physical marks: Scratches, phone wallpapers, brand names, or distinctive keychains help prove ownership without ambiguity.
  • Keep contact channels open: Ensure your email or phone number is active to receive claim notifications immediately.

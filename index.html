Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the campus lost & found system.

What is FindIT?

FindIT is a centralized campus web platform designed for students, faculty, and administrative staff to report lost personal items, list found belongings, and coordinate prompt on-campus returns.

Who can use FindIT?

Any registered student, faculty member, or staff member with valid college credentials at KIET Deemed To Be University can create an account and participate in the system.

How do I report a lost item?

Sign in with your campus account and navigate to Report Lost. Fill out the item name, category, campus location, date, and description. You can optionally upload a reference photograph to assist finders.

How do I report a found item?

Sign in and click Report Found. Provide details about the item and indicate where you have deposited it (e.g. Campus Security Cabin at Gate 1 or Central Library Reception Desk).

How can I claim an item?

Open the item details page while signed in and click Claim Item. You will be prompted to submit proof of ownership (such as lock screen wallpaper, specific card names, or unique physical scratches) that only the real owner knows.

Do I need an account to access item listings?

Yes. To protect student privacy, prevent false claims, and keep contact details confidential, all item listings are restricted to authenticated campus members. Visitors must sign in or register to browse listings.
